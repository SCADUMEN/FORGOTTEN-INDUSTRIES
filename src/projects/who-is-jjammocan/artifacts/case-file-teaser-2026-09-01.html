Who Is J.J. Ammo Can?

A wide-brimmed hat. A pair of goggles. No name, no face on record, no pattern anyone's pinned down yet — only sightings, filed one city at a time.

Identifying marks

  • Double snap brim, 3″ Wide-brim sun hat, snapped up at two points along the front edge.
  • Pinched front crown, 4½″ Center crease runs front to back, pinched rather than domed.
  • Cotton canvas construction Reported in khaki, green, distressed gold, earth, and black.
  • Goggles, always worn Every report to date describes them — no sighting without.
