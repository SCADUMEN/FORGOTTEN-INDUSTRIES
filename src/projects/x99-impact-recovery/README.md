# LE RÉDEMPTEURE / X99 Recovery Dossier

Project id: `FI-PROJ-009`

Public route: `/projects/x99-impact-recovery/`

## Purpose

This dossier recovers the MSI X99S XPOWER AC and i7-5930K as the historically
intended compute platform for the CaseLabs Mercury S8. It begins before
diagnosis or reconstruction, while preserving the longer operating lineage now
established by recovered social records, physical intake, and direct operator
testimony. The separately retained ASUS Impact assembly remains in the record as
an adjacent hardware artifact.

The record does not assume that any platform works. The MSI X99 assembly operated in the 2014 test-bench system before developing unresolved boot and configuration problems. The operator replaced it rather than completing fault isolation. The ASUS assemblies have not yet received controlled bench tests. The water-cooling parts are treated as separate pressure-test subjects until their internal condition is known.

## Platform A / MSI X99

- Motherboard: MSI X99S XPOWER AC.
- Socket and memory: LGA2011-v3 / eight DDR4 slots.
- Processor: Intel Core i7-5930K / S-spec SR20R / 3.50 GHz / installed.
- Processor identity: confirmed by direct heat-spreader reading in an operator-supplied inspection photograph received July 18, 2026.
- Known history: the platform was operating by December 2014. It later became difficult to configure and would not boot reliably; no historical diagnosis established an electrical failure.
- Present physical state, July 30, 2026: motherboard stored in its plastic tray; processor still seated and socket latched; old thermal paste preliminarily removed from the heat spreader; eight DIMM slots and zero installed memory modules.
- Present electrical state: no power-on has occurred during the current recovery pass. Function remains completely unverified. Do not represent the platform as working or dead.

## Systems lineage / operator chronology

This chronology joins dated public records to the operator's direct account. It records succession and historical operation; it does not establish the present function of any recovered component.

- **Predecessor / provenance gap — ASUS Rampage IV Black Edition / X79.** The
  operator identifies this presently missing board as the predecessor and
  planned prior platform. No claim is made that it was physically installed in
  the Mercury S8 without confirming evidence.
- **December 2014 — X99 enters service.** Recovered records document Kingston DDR4-3000, MSI GTX 970 Gold hardware, and the operating open test bench. The operator identifies the visible motherboard as the same MSI X99S XPOWER AC now registered as `FI-HW-MB-X99-001` and as the historically intended successor compute platform for the Mercury S8.
- **February 2016 — X99 exit path.** The recovered February 23 post identifies the continuing test-bench configuration as MSI X99 XPOWER AC / Intel Core i7-5930K / 16 GB Kingston DDR4-3000 / SLI MSI GTX 970 Gold. The operator now records that the platform had become too complicated to stabilize and would not boot reliably. The exact fault remains unresolved.
- **February through May 2016 — 6700K / REDEEMER.** An Intel Core i7-6700K on ASUS Z170-Deluxe appears in the recovered record on February 20. The replacement platform became REDEEMER, the white NZXT H440 system formally documented with the 6700K in May 2016.
- **2017 — 7700K successor.** An Intel Core i7-7700K system followed REDEEMER as the operator's next PC. The operator places this succession in 2017, consistent with the processor's Q1 2017 launch; its exact motherboard, enclosure, adoption date, and configuration history remain to be reconciled against source evidence.
- **Approximately mid-July 2026 — 7700K handoff.** After the JJAMMOCAN material exchange established a new Ryzen test-bench position, the 7700K system was transferred to a new owner. The exact date remains pending; price, counterparty, and handoff details remain outside the public record.
- **July 2026 — JJAMMOCAN / Ryzen 7600.** An iPad Mini exchange supplied a SAMA micro-ATX case, motherboard, Ryzen 5 7600, 8 GB test memory, RTX 5060, and Thermaltake 600 W power supply for the new bench position. This is the continuity platform while X99 is recovered, not the permanent destination.
- **By July 29, 2026 — JJAMMOCAN changes state.** The RTX 5060 was exchanged for a GTX 1060 6 GB and a TeamGroup 16 GB DDR5 kit. The public `FI-LOG-016` record preserves the resulting single-module memory-validation gate.
- **Continuance gate.** Do not sell or dismantle the AM5 platform after a single
  X99 POST. JJAMMOCAN remains intact until LE RÉDEMPTEURE demonstrates
  repeatable cold boots, stable firmware recognition, memory validation, and
  stock thermal and load stability.

## Recovery sequence

### Preserve

1. Photograph both sides of each board before cleaning.
2. Record the confirmed X99 processor markings without publishing batch or serial markings by default.
3. Keep the installed 5930K seated and the X99 socket latched. Do not expose the LGA socket merely for inspection.
4. Record every serial or manufacturing label locally; do not publish it by default.
5. Before any power-on, move the X99 board from its plastic storage tray to a clean cardboard motherboard box or another rigid, nonconductive bench surface. Never power a motherboard atop an antistatic bag.

### Isolate

1. Inspect the visible X99 board surfaces for heat discoloration, damaged power stages, missing components, conductive residue, and abnormal odor without removing the seated processor.
2. Inspect the ASUS socket before introducing a processor.
3. Keep both liquid-cooling blocks outside any live computer loop until cleaned and pressure-tested.

### Minimum bench / X99

1. Clean cardboard motherboard box or another rigid, nonconductive bench surface. Do not power the board in its plastic tray or atop an antistatic bag.
2. Seated i7-5930K with a compatible LGA2011-v3 air cooler and fresh thermal
   paste.
3. One known-good non-ECC unbuffered DDR4 UDIMM at a standard JEDEC setting in `DIMM1`.
4. Use the non-modular Thermaltake 600 W only after its exact model label, age,
   fixed leads, and physical condition are cleared. Use its native 24-pin ATX,
   CPU/EPS 8-pin, and GPU PCIe leads. Do not substitute a PCIe lead for CPU/EPS.
5. Use the air-cooled GTX 1060 6 GB; the i7-5930K does not provide integrated
   graphics. Do not power the fully waterblocked GTX 780 dry.
6. Keyboard and display only as required to reach firmware.
7. With incoming power disconnected, clear CMOS. Use the motherboard's onboard power control for the first attempt and record the two-digit diagnostic code.
8. Exclude drives, M.2, Wi-Fi, networking, liquid blocks, pumps, BIOS flashing,
   overclocking, and permanent installation from the first pass. Preserve the
   unused but unverified AX1500i. Keep loose modular cables unassigned until
   exact PSU provenance is established; never mix modular PSU cables.
9. Stop immediately for smoke, visible arcing, sharp electrical odor, or abnormal heating.

### Minimum bench / ASUS

1. Inspect and clear the socket first.
2. Use a low-risk known-good LGA1150 processor, compatible air cooler, and one known-good DDR3 module.
3. Clear CMOS and enter firmware only. No storage writes or permanent assembly.
4. Record CPU identification, memory detection, fan speed, temperatures, and any diagnostic state.

## Restoration gates

- **Role** — X99 / 5930K is the intended compute platform for LE RÉDEMPTEURE,
  not a secondary experimental machine. Its role is established; present
  function is not.
- **Cooling** — visible Intel LGA 2011-labeled studs and EK hardware do not
  establish a complete first-power cooler. The test waits for a confirmed
  compatible air-cooler heatsink, fan, complete mounting set, and fresh paste.
- **Memory** — loose DDR4 custody is established, but one module must still be
  identified from its label and verified as compatible, known-good, non-ECC
  unbuffered DDR4 before installation in `DIMM1`.
- **Power** — 600 W is sufficient nominal capacity for the stock 5930K and GTX
  1060 bench set, but the Thermaltake supply's exact model, age, fixed leads, and
  condition must be recorded before power.
- **LE CONTINUANT / continuance** — a first POST authorizes further staged
  validation, not the sale of JJAMMOCAN. The AM5 continuity platform remains
  intact until X99 stability is proven beyond one POST.
- **Archive** — LE SAUVEGARDER's arrays remain planned until each drive,
  carrier, controller, power path, and independent archive copy is reconciled.

## Claim boundary

- `identified` means the board marking or operator identification is recorded.
- `untested` means no present working claim exists and no power-on has occurred during the current recovery pass.
- `known prior failure` means unresolved historical boot and configuration failure. It does not mean that an electrical fault was established.
- `eight DIMM slots` describes motherboard capacity, not installed memory. The recovered X99 board presently contains zero RAM modules; all earlier populated-memory bandwidth figures were hypothetical.
- `loose DDR4` establishes visible custody only. It does not establish capacity,
  speed, compatibility, or function.
- `L’ARCHIVE` names the completed physical artwork as a whole. It does not mean
  that the cube, pedestal, loop, or arrays are presently assembled or working.
- `monoblock` refers to the EK-FB ASUS M6I CPU, Z87 PCH, and VRM/MOSFET cooling artifact retained for the Maximus VI Impact. The GTX 660 Ti carries a separate full-cover GPU block.
- The X99 CPU identity is confirmed. Its function, the exact EVGA graphics-card
  variant, the EK-FC670 GTX material variant, and mounting-hardware completeness
  remain unverified.
