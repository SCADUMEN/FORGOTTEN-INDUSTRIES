## I. The Principle

Facebook Marketplace is not a store.

It is a weather system.

A man opens the app looking for one useful object - maybe a shelf, maybe a storage box, maybe a single replacement part - and suddenly the sky changes.

The winds shift.

The shithawks are circling.

## II. Core Definitions

### The Shithawk

A local acquisition hazard disguised as a deal.

Not necessarily bad. Not necessarily useless. But always carrying hidden costs: space, time, gas, cables, repair work, emotional energy, or a new project branch.

### Shithawk Containment Infrastructure

Any purchase that reduces chaos instead of multiplying it.

Examples:

- standardized metal cans
- shelves
- label makers
- drawer units
- inventory sheets
- zip bags
- desiccant packs
- QR labels
- field logs

The green metal cans are not shithawks.

The cans are shithawk containment units.

## III. Shithawk Taxonomy

### Storage Shithawk

Appears as bins, trunks, cabinets, shelves, ammo cans, filing drawers.

Usually good, but must be tested with one question:

Will this impose order, or merely hold disorder?

Verdict on green cans: APPROVED. CONTAINMENT-GRADE.

### Tool Shithawk

The tool says: "You will need me someday."

Sometimes true. Often a trap.

A $20 specialty tool can become a three-week tool-restoration subproject.

Rule: buy only if it solves an active bottleneck.

### Electronics Shithawk

Usually arrives missing:

- one cable
- one screw
- one proprietary adapter
- one driver disc
- one truth

Danger level: high.

Phrase of warning:

"Works, just haven't tested it."

Translation:

"The shithawks own this now."

### Artifact Shithawk

Beautiful. Useless. Magnetic.

The GE Blue Max transistor-looking thing is the purest form.

It does not solve a problem.

It creates a justification field around itself.

FI may acquire artifacts, but only intentionally.

### Free-Item Shithawk

The most expensive shithawk of all.

Price: $0

Cost: two hours, one awkward porch pickup, half a tank of gas, and a new pile in the garage.

Rule:

Free is not free unless it enters the system immediately.

### Car Shithawk

A separate ecosystem.

Do not engage casually.

Cars on Marketplace are not listings.

They are portals.

Rule:

No car portal without a sober adult, daylight, a mechanic mindset, and a defined exit strategy.

### Side-Quest Shithawk

The deadliest species.

It begins with:

"That would be easy to fix."

It ends three weeks later with 17 browser tabs, missing screws, and a new vocabulary of obscure parts.

Rule:

No side quests during containment operations.

## VII. Inspection Checklist

Before purchase, inspect:

- lids latch cleanly
- seals are intact or at least serviceable
- no major rust inside
- no oil / chemical / mildew smell
- no sharp damaged seams
- most cans are the same size
- stackability is consistent
- seller will honor bulk rate

Bring:

- gloves
- flashlight
- cash
- towel / blanket for transport
- one sample small-part bag if desired
