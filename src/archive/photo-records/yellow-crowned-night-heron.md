## RECORD SCOPE

This plate consolidates the eleven public JPEG derivatives registered as
[FI-PHOTO-004](/archive/objects/fi-photo-004/) and
[FI-PHOTO-005](/archive/objects/fi-photo-005/). The photographs document a
juvenile bird at eye level and along a roofline, one adult between two juveniles
in a nest frame, and one later juvenile observed at ground level.

The archive records the working identification as **yellow-crowned night heron
(_Nyctanassa violacea_)**. Individual identity is not established across the
three encounters. Exact site, GPS data, residence-relative context, and source
metadata remain withheld.

FIG. 02 is published only as a redacted derivative. The black bars are baked
into the public file; access to the unobscured source requires C2 clearance.

## RESEARCH USE // AUGUST 2026

This plate may be used as a visual reference for an August 2026 Wichita avian
research proposal. A defensible review should:

1. cross-check species and age class in each frame against current field marks;
2. distinguish what the photographs show from what they cannot establish;
3. propose a repeatable, low-disturbance observation method for urban or
   suburban birds in Wichita;
4. avoid inferring individual identity, parentage, nest success, or exact site;
5. preserve the field rule: observe, do not handle, do not feed, and do not
   disturb the nest.

Potential study variables include observation time, age class, substrate or
perch type, height class, behavior, group count, canopy cover, and distance from
the observer. Any August observations should be logged as a new source set
rather than added retroactively to these July records.
