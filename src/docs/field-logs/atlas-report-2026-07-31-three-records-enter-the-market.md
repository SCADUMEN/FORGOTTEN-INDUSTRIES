## Evidence Chain

The work began as photographic intake rather than marketplace copy.

- Original photographs remained untouched.
- Listing derivatives were separated by object and ordered as condition
  evidence.
- Public JPEGs were normalized for legibility and cleared of source metadata.
- Crop, rotation, exposure, and color correction did not remove scratches,
  shelf wear, labels, or disc-surface evidence.
- The Skyrim product-key image was redacted before it entered the buyer-facing
  set.

The result was not three generic descriptions attached to photographs. Each
listing was assembled from the contents visibly present in its own evidence
packet.

## Claim Boundary

The two PlayStation 3 discs were described as tested and working only after the
operator confirmed the test state. Fallout: New Vegas was listed without its
original manual, and the absence was stated in the title and description.

The Skyrim disc was recorded as readable. Its original Steam product-key
sticker remains with the package, but activation status is unknown. The listing
does not guarantee an unused, reusable, or transferable key.

Automated catalog suggestions were treated as provisional. A false
manual-included suggestion was removed from the New Vegas record. A false
one-month manufacturer warranty was removed from the Skyrim record.

## Machine Collaboration / Human Release

ATLAS separated the image sets, assembled searchable titles, drafted condition
and contents disclosures, audited catalog specifics, calibrated parcel profiles,
preserved buyer-paid shipping, and checked each buyer-facing preview.

The operator supplied the facts that the machine could not infer: test state,
packaging constraint, acquisition context, and final judgment. Each listing
stopped at the release control until the operator inspected it. Publication
remained a human action.
