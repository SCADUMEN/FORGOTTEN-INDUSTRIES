## À Propos Draft

Forgotten Industries began in the 2014-era enthusiast PC world: custom systems, watercooling hardware, obsolete components, specialized tools, and the strange material culture of the pre-Amazon internet.

Over a decade later, in long-term recovery from substance use disorder and after scientific, classical, and medical training, he returned to the archive with a different eye.

What was once mainly acquisition and assembly had become something more deliberate: classification, documentation, restoration, triage, and preservation.

The old materials are still here.

The operator is not the same.

L'OPÉRATEUR HAS RETURNED WITH TRAINING.

Forgotten Industries is now an archive of systems: machines, parts, texts, methods, failures, recoveries, and the infrastructure required to keep memory from dissolving into piles.

A thing documented is a thing not yet lost.

## More Personal Version

Forgotten Industries began as a hardware project during the 2014 enthusiast PC era. It was built from watercooling parts, cases, cables, radiators, fittings, tools, and the obsessive supply chains of a very different internet.

Over a decade later, in long-term recovery from substance use disorder and after years of scientific, classical, and medical training, he came back to what remained.

The work had changed.

It was no longer only about building machines. It was about recognizing what survived, sorting what matters, documenting what can be known, and giving structure to the things that would otherwise disappear into storage, memory, or waste.

L'OPÉRATEUR HAS RETURNED WITH TRAINING.

Forgotten Industries is the continuation of that work: an archive, a workshop, a field log, and a system for preserving the material evidence of a life rebuilt around attention, judgment, and care.
