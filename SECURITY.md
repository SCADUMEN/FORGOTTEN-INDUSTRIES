# Security Policy

Forgotten Industries is a static public archive. Security reports should concern the deployed site, its release pipeline, or an unintended disclosure from the repository or generated output.

## Reporting

Send reports to [LESAUVEGARDER@GMAIL.COM](mailto:LESAUVEGARDER@GMAIL.COM?subject=Security%20report). Include the affected URL, a concise reproduction, expected impact, and any evidence that can be shared safely.

Do not include passwords, private keys, client identities, home addresses, or unredacted restricted records in an issue or pull request. Do not perform denial-of-service testing, destructive testing, social engineering, or testing against third-party accounts.

## Restricted records

Restricted briefing routes publish encrypted ciphertext for browser-local decryption. They are not server-side identity systems. Access credentials are distributed separately, retained outside Git, and rotated when their handling boundary changes.

## Response

Reports will be acknowledged when received, reproduced against the owning source or platform, and resolved through the normal verified release path. Public disclosure should wait until remediation is live or a coordinated date is agreed.
