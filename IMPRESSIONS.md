# Reader Impressions Register

This register catalogues real-world human responses to Forgotten Industries. It is evidence, not promotional copy.

## Rules

- Record only feedback a person actually gave.
- Preserve exact words separately from any editorial summary.
- Do not infer a name, role, relationship, or sentiment.
- Keep private contact details and unredacted correspondence in local intake.
- Publish a quotation or identity only after explicit clearance.
- Record criticism, confusion, and disagreement with the same care as praise.
- Note what changed because of the response; do not turn a reaction into a testimonial by default.

## Intake record

Use one identifier per response: `FI-IMP-YYYY-NNN`.

Each local intake record should preserve:

- date received;
- medium and reading context;
- reader name or chosen alias;
- exact response;
- concise operator summary;
- affected page, dossier, or object;
- resulting correction, question, or decision;
- evidence location;
- public-clearance state: `NO`, `ANONYMOUS`, or `ATTRIBUTED`;
- date and method of consent when public clearance is not `NO`.

Only the public-safe fields move into this register. Source messages and identifying metadata remain local unless the reader explicitly authorizes publication.
